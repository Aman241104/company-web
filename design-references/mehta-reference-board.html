What changed

  • The warm cream / forest green / coral palette is gone, replaced by Cloud, Midnight and Mehta Cobalt across every page.

Agency sites to learn from

These are picked for craft and for how they sell to international buyers, not for size. Each card says what to take from the site. We borrow the principle, never the layout or the copy. The lime tag marks my top three.

Step 4 · what this means for our plan

Recommendations

Patterns across competitors

  • Almost every one now leads with "AI-native" or "AI-powered", so that alone no longer stands out.
  • They sell on scale (years, headcount, client counts). We can't win that, and shouldn't fake it.
  • Most sites are dense and corporate. A clean, branded site is a real edge with design-aware buyers abroad.
  • Very few show public pricing. Our fixed pricing is rare and worth keeping.

Changes I'd make for v3 (international)

  1. Rewrite the homepage headline and meta for global buyers, using the brand line "Technology that grows your business".
  2. Add USD pricing and state time-zone overlap (IST working hours vs US and UK).
  3. Turn the 3–4 best projects into full case studies: challenge, approach, result with real metrics.
  4. Give Vibo ERP its own product page (Linear-style), as proof that we build software and not only websites.
  5. Add services × industries pages for SEO, following Netguru's structure. Only list industries we have actually worked in.
  6. Get a Clutch / GoodFirms profile with real reviews. Buyers abroad check these.
Need from you

Decisions before we start v3

  1. Which 2–3 references from Step 2 should set the direction? (My suggestion: Composite, Monterail and Evil Martians.)
  2. Which countries do we target first: US, UK, UAE or Australia? This changes the pricing currency and the copy.
  3. Can we publish client names, metrics and testimonials for the case studies? We need written OK from each client.
  4. Do we have, or plan, a non-India address or phone number to show on the site?
